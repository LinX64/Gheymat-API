Gheymat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Gheymat is an app for iPhone, Apple Watch and Android that shows Iranian free-market currency, gold and crypto prices. This policy covers the Gheymat app on every platform, including its widgets.

Data we collect

None. Gheymat has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK. The app does not know who you are and cannot identify you.

Data stored on your device

Your favourites, your chosen list/grid layout, your appearance and price-unit settings, your price alerts, and a local cache of the latest prices are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. Home-screen widgets and the Apple Watch app read the same on-device data. This data never leaves your device and is not visible to us.

Notifications and permissions

Price alerts are checked on your device and shown as local notifications; your alerts are never sent anywhere. On Android the app asks for the notifications permission only when you create your first alert, and uses internet access only to fetch prices.

Network requests

The app fetches currency, gold and crypto prices from Gheymat's own data feed. These requests carry no account identifier, advertising identifier, or other data that identifies you. Standard network metadata (such as your IP address) is visible to the hosting infrastructure the way it is for any web request, and is not logged or retained by us in a way that is linked to you.

Price data source

Prices come from public Iranian market data. Gheymat is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any market or exchange.

Children

Gheymat is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Material changes will be reflected in the app's release notes.
